NUSD / sNUSD · redemptions since the freeze

Neutrl froze on Aug 13.
Who has redeemed since?

Neutrl is a synthetic-dollar protocol (NUSD, staked as sNUSD). It froze NUSD and sNUSD on Aug 13, 2026 after a reserve shortfall. Redemptions reopened Sep 17 at a fixed 0.51 USDC per NUSD and are expected to close Nov 14. This tracker uses onchain data, updated hourly, to show how many freeze-snapshot holders have redeemed so far. What happened ↗

strataStrata senior vs junior (srNUSD / jrNUSD)

Strata tranches Neutrl sNUSD into a senior (srNUSD) and first-loss junior (jrNUSD). After Neutrl's 0.51 rate, onchain accounting wrote the junior claim to ~0 and left residual strategy sNUSD covering senior. Strata withdrawals settle in sNUSD/NUSD — not Neutrl portal USDC. Deposits are disabled onchain.

protocolsHeld through protocols at the snapshot (not counted as wallets)

These contracts hold NUSD/sNUSD on behalf of their own users (Pendle PT/YT/LP, Strata srNUSD/jrNUSD, Morpho/Euler lenders, DEX pools, bridges). Those end users are affected, but this tracker does not break these contracts down to individual users. The exception is Neutrl's AssetLock: each locker is counted as a wallet instead.

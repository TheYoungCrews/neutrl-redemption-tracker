Neutrl Redemption Tracker

Neutrl is a synthetic-dollar protocol (NUSD, staked as sNUSD). It froze NUSD and sNUSD on Aug 13, 2026 after a reserve shortfall. Redemptions reopened Sep 17 at a fixed 0.51 USDC per NUSD and are expected to close Nov 14. This tracker shows, onchain and updated hourly, how many holders from the freeze snapshot have redeemed so far. What happened ↗
